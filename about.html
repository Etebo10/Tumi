Founder & CEO

Tumi

Tumi started in Dubai property the way most honest advisors do — frustrated by how the industry usually treats international buyers as a signature to collect, not a relationship to build. Dubai Real Estate Services was built to flip that: one advisor per client, full transparency on fees and forecasts, and a team that stays on after the keys are handed over.

Today Tumi and the team work almost exclusively with buyers based outside the UAE — investors who need a Dubai-based partner they can trust to view a unit, question a developer, and negotiate in person on their behalf.
