The Golden Visa Route

One purchase, ten years of residency.

A qualifying property investment of AED 2,000,000 or more can make you and your immediate family eligible for a renewable 10-year UAE Golden Visa — no local sponsor required.

  • Minimum AED 2M investment

    Can be a single unit or a combination of properties that meet the threshold.

  • Family-inclusive

    Spouse and children can typically be added to the same residency application.

  • Renewable, not one-time

    The visa renews as long as the qualifying property is retained.
