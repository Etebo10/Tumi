Why Dubai

A market built for owners, not just tourists.

Zero property tax, freehold ownership for foreign buyers, and a currency pegged to the dollar make Dubai one of the few global cities where the numbers and the lifestyle point the same direction.

  • 0% property & capital gains tax

    What you earn on a Dubai asset is what you keep — no annual property tax, no capital gains tax on resale.

  • Golden Visa from AED 2M

    A qualifying purchase can unlock a 10-year renewable UAE residency for you and your family.

  • Freehold ownership

    Non-UAE nationals can hold full, transferable title in designated freehold zones — Downtown, Marina, Palm Jumeirah and more.
